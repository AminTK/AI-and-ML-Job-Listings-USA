# AI-and-ML-Job-Listings-USA
### Compiled Job Postings from 2022 to 2024

## Dataset Overview
The dataset titled “AI and ML Job Listings in the United States” offers a comprehensive collection of job postings in the fields of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML) across the U.S. Spanning from 2022 to 2024, the dataset captures the evolving landscape of AI/ML job opportunities. It serves as a valuable resource for researchers, job seekers, and data scientists interested in analyzing trends, demands, and opportunities within the AI/ML job market.

### Applications in Data Science
This dataset can be utilized for various data science applications, including:

1. Trend Analysis: Identifying changes in job titles, locations, and required skills over time.
2. Demand Forecasting: Predicting future demand for AI/ML roles based on historical data.
3. Skill Gap Analysis: Analyzing the level of skills and experience in demand compared to the available workforce.
4. Geospatial Analysis: Mapping job opportunities across different regions of the United States.
5. Salary Prediction: Developing models to estimate salaries based on job descriptions and other features.Some job descriptions include compensation-related information, which can be identified by analyzing the "Description" column for references to salary, pay, or compensation terms.

## Column Descriptions
- Title: The job title (e.g., AI/ML Engineer).
- Location: The job location (e.g., New York, NY).
- Published At: The date the job was posted (e.g., 2024-05-29).
- Company Name: The name of the company posting the job (e.g., Wesper).
- Description: A detailed description of the job, including responsibilities, qualifications, and sometimes salary information.
- Applications Count: The number of applications received (e.g., over 200 applicants).
- Contract Type: The nature of the contract (e.g., Full-time).
- Experience Level: The level of experience required (e.g., Mid-level).
- Work Type: The type of work involved (e.g., Engineering and Information Technology).
- Sector: The industry sector of the job (e.g., Online Publishing).
